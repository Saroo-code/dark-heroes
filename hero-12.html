OVERVIEW ◆

Bloodmoon appeared during the longest lunar eclipse in the history of the Dark Universe. From that night, an ancient lunar energy awakened within her.

She learned to control the power of the moon, using its energy to enhance her speed, strength, and combat abilities. When the blood moon rises, her true power is unleashed.

“The moon watches everything. I simply learned how to make it answer.”
