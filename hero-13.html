OVERVIEW ◆

Ash Reaper emerged from the ruins of a kingdom consumed by an ancient fire. The flames destroyed his home, but left behind a mysterious power hidden within the ashes.

He learned to control the remaining energy, transforming ash and fire into weapons and armor. Now he travels through the Dark Universe, leaving only silence and ash behind.

“The flames may fade. But the ash always remembers.”
