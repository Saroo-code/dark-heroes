OVERVIEW ◆

Frostbane emerged from the frozen lands beyond the northern kingdoms, where endless winter consumed everything in its path.

He learned to control dark ice, shaping frozen energy into weapons, armor, and devastating attacks. His connection with the frozen realm grows stronger with every battle.
