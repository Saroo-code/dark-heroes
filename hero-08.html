OVERVIEW ◆

Ember Knight was forged in the heart of a ruined kingdom where ancient flames consumed the land and left nothing but ash behind.

He learned to command dark flame, transforming destructive fire into weapons and armor. His burning energy grows stronger whenever he enters the battlefield.

“Every flame begins with a spark. Mine began with darkness.”
