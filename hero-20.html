OVERVIEW ◆

Eclipse is the final guardian of the Dark Universe, born during a legendary event when the forces of light and darkness collided.

Unlike other heroes, Eclipse can command both opposing energies. Shadow gives him control over darkness, while light allows him to restore balance between the two forces.

“True power is not choosing between light and darkness. It is mastering both.”
