OVERVIEW ◆

Dark Nova was discovered beyond the edge of known space, where an ancient cosmic energy source had been drifting through the universe for thousands of years.

After absorbing a fragment of this energy, Dark Nova gained control over cosmic forces. He can channel stellar energy, create powerful energy waves, and manipulate the space around him.
