OVERVIEW ◆

Silent Blade was trained by the mysterious Silent Order, a secret organization that has protected the Dark Universe from the shadows for generations.

Unlike other warriors, Silent Blade relies on speed, precision, and stealth rather than raw power. He can disappear into darkness and move through enemy territory without being detected.

“The strongest attack is the one your enemy never sees.”
