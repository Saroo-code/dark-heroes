OVERVIEW ◆

Hellforged was once a warrior who disappeared beneath the ruins of an ancient battlefield. Years later, he returned carrying the power of the infernal realm within him.

His armor was forged through supernatural flames, while his greatsword became a vessel for destructive infernal energy. Every battle makes his power burn brighter.

“Strength is not born. It is forged through fire.”
