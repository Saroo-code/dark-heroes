OVERVIEW ◆

Thunder Veil was born beneath a sky consumed by endless storms, where lightning became the source of his extraordinary power.

He learned to command storm energy, shaping lightning into weapons and moving with the speed of a thunderbolt. Every battle makes the storm inside him stronger.

“When the sky goes silent, the storm is only getting closer.”
