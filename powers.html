POWER CLASSIFICATION

Not every awakened power carries the same level of danger. The DARK HEROES system classifies abilities according to their origin, control, and destructive potential.

CLASS I

AWAKENED

Basic supernatural abilities with limited range and controlled energy output.

CLASS II

ENHANCED

Advanced abilities capable of changing the outcome of a serious battle.

CLASS III

DOMINANT

Rare powers that can overwhelm multiple opponents and alter the battlefield.

CLASS IV

FORBIDDEN

Extremely rare powers whose full potential remains unknown to the DARK HEROES system.
