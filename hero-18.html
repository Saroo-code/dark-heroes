OVERVIEW ◆

Mist Walker appeared in the forgotten cities hidden beyond the borders of the Dark Universe, where a mysterious mist covers the land.

He learned to merge his body with the dark mist, becoming almost impossible to detect. He can disappear into the fog, move between shadows, and strike before his enemies realize he is there.

“The mist has no beginning. The mist has no end. Neither do I.”
