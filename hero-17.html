OVERVIEW ◆

Storm Reaper emerged from the heart of a supernatural storm that destroyed an ancient fortress and awakened the power of lightning within him.

He learned to control electrical energy, calling lightning from the sky and channeling it through his weapon. His connection with the storm grows stronger with every battle.

“Thunder is only the warning. The lightning is the judgment.”
